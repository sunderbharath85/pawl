Your agents work. Your pets watch.

Pawl gives every coding agent on every machine a named pet. It naps while nothing runs, digs while the agent works, and barks the moment it needs your OK.

Download for Windows Read the source

Free and open source. Click the wheel to swap pets.

Why the name

A pawl is the small catch that lets a wheel turn one way and stops it from slipping back. Your agents move forward on their own. The risky step waits, with a click, for you.

One pet for every agent, on every machine you own.

Four kinds of pet

Name them, pick a species and give them a personality. Each one acts out what its agent is doing.

idle

Approve right from the pet

A speech bubble with Approve and Deny. No window to find, no tab to switch.

Rules you write

  • allow reads in the repo
  • ask before edits
  • deny always wins

Sealed end to end

Your devices talk in NaCl boxes. The relay you deploy only passes sealed envelopes along.

A session, as your pet sees it

Every agent on every machine gets a pet. Its mood is the session's status, at a glance.

Naps

Nothing running. Your pet curls up on its cushion and the strip stays quiet.

Digs

The agent edits files and runs tools. Your pet digs right along, and the feed shows every step.

Barks

A command needs your OK. Approve from the pet, the panel, VS Code or the CLI, on whichever machine is closest.

Visits

Pair your machines and their pets come over. A session on your laptop walks onto your desktop strip.

Wherever you are working

Desktop

A transparent strip where your pets walk, plus a panel for sessions, chat, approvals, rules and every setting.

CLI

One pawl binary runs the hub and answers from the terminal.

$ pawl pending
ID        MACHINE  AGENT   TOOL      WAITING
7f3a91c2  Desk PC  codex   bun test  12s ago
c04e5b18  Laptop   claude  bun test  40s ago

$ pawl approve 7f3a
Approved: bun test

VS Code

Pets in the status bar, approvals in a webview, and one click to open the files an agent edited. Built from the repo for now.

Phone

In development: pair by QR code, reach every machine through your relay, and approve with Face ID or a fingerprint.

Relay

Away from home? Deploy your own relay on Cloudflare in minutes. It is closed by an access key and never sees inside a message.
